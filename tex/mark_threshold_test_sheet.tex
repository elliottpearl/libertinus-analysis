\section*{Test sheet: the mark-axis rule, threshold 10 or exact}
The patched fonts apply the mark-axis rule to every symmetric above mark: where its $k$ is 10 or more from $K$ (0; 150 in semibold italic), its anchor X is moved onto its axis ($k = K$).
The question is whether the threshold should be lower, for very symmetric marks such as the ring, where a few units may show.
``Rule'' rows are the patched fonts (threshold 10); ``exact'' rows are the patched fonts with every symmetric above mark exactly on its axis, curated anchors included (threshold 0).
{\color{precomposed}Blue}: the designer's precomposed letter (not changed). Black: base + mark, placed by the anchors.
$k$ is now measured on the mark's ink (its bounds with the slant removed).
